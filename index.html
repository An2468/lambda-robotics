01 / OUR IDENTITY

WE DON'TJUST BUILD.WE BECOME.

Lambda is ten students in Ho Chi Minh City who decided not to wait for permission. No school program, no budget, no shop handed to us — we found the space, taught ourselves CAD, learned to weld a chassis that survives a match, and wrote the code that drives it. This is our first FIRST Tech Challenge season. We intend to be hard to forget.

Independent and self-funded. Every tool, every part and every hour of build time comes from the ten of us — and from the partners who decide to back us.
