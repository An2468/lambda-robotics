WE DON'TJUST BUILD.WE BECOME.

Ten students in Ho Chi Minh City. No school program. No budget. No workshop. We found the space, taught ourselves CAD, and built a robot that competes. First season. We plan to be hard to forget.

Independent and self-funded. Every tool and every part comes from the ten of us, and from the partners who back us.
